Muse

Muse is an English rock band from Teignmouth, Devon, formed in 1994: Matthew Bellamy on vocals, guitar and piano, Christopher Wolstenholme on bass and Dominic Howard on drums. Their sound runs from alternative and progressive rock into electronica and classical music, carried by Bellamy's falsetto and their huge stadium shows. They've won two Grammys for Best Rock Album, for The Resistance and Drones.
